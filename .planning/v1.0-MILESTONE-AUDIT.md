## Issues Found and Resolved During Audit

1. **CRITICAL (Fixed):** `_execute_btc_order` and `_execute_roll` in `src/telegram/bot.py` were calling `preview_options_order`/`place_options_order` with 7 arguments instead of 10. The OCC symbol string was passed in the `expiry_year` slot. Fixed by parsing `expiry_date` from position dict and passing full 10-arg signature. Commit: `8026d4b`.

2. **Minor:** `compute_cycle_pnl()` in `src/database.py` is orphaned — never called by consumers. Dashboard and `/wheel` read `realized_pnl` directly from cycle dict.
